
## S14 COST MATH
Now the money. One run like that costs roughly one orchestrator brain plus three cheap hands — call it eleven minutes of mixed models. Run it twenty times a month and the team still costs less than two hours of top-model solo time, because workers never touch the expensive tier. Repeatable work belongs to teams. One-offs belong to the smartest solo. Route by that rule and the bill stays flat while output multiplies.

## S15 FIVE MISTAKES
Five mistakes kill teams. One: the orchestrator executes instead of handing out. Two: sideways handoffs, workers briefing workers. Three: no caps, sixty messages for one task. Four: untrimmed tools, including the twenty-minute self-delegation loop. Five: skipping the debrief, so next week repeats this week. All five are configuration, not intelligence. Fix the config, keep the models.

## S16 SOLO VS TEAM
So when solo, when team? Solo for the one-off judgment call: pricing strategy, the final edit, anything decided once. Team for everything repeatable: scans, drafts, checks, posts, packs. If you'll do it three times, card it. If you'll do it ten times, cron it. That is the whole operating system in two sentences.
